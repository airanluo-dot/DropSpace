Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 permits reasonable and customary use of the DropSpace name when describing the origin of the software and when reproducing the project's NOTICE. Descriptive statements such as “Forked from DropSpace” and “Based on DropSpace” are allowed when accurate.

The DropSpace name, official logo, application icon, and other source-identifying brand elements identify the official DropSpace project and its releases. A modified or independently distributed version must not use them in a way that is likely to imply sponsorship, endorsement, or official status. Distributors should clearly identify material modifications and use distinct branding where necessary to avoid confusion.

This policy concerns trademark and source-identification uses only. It does not add restrictions to the copyright and patent permissions granted for the source code and brand image files by Apache-2.0.

Questions about an intended brand use may be raised through the repository's GitHub issue tracker. No separate permission is needed for truthful nominative or descriptive references.
